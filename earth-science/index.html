100 levels of stealth extract — not a quiz skin. Tap a unit — then choose a skill (or All) and start. Questions follow the 2018 Virginia Earth Science Standards of Learning (ES.1–ES.12); each one is tagged with its skill and whether it is a lower-order (LOTS) or higher-order (HOTS) thinking skill. Move quiet, grab the right letter on the map, extract at EXIT · SAFE. Correct letters call Sol's CHARIOT. START · SAFE is where you spawn; EXIT · SAFE is where you extract and read. Hati cannot see you in either booth. Catches and wrong letters both cost a life; three (then two) end a level. Win to unlock the next level on this Chromebook. Itch login does not store progress. Solo works without a class code.
