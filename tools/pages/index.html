Sol's Labyrinth · SOL review game

A reading-practice maze for Chromebooks. Read the passage, grab the right letter, walk it to the exit before the wolves catch you, and build your castle with the coins you earn. Progress saves on your Chromebook.

Tips. Play in the same browser every time so your levels, coins and castle are kept. Use the ⤢ button in the castle builder to fit the whole build, the mouse wheel to turn it, and right-click a piece to turn that piece.
