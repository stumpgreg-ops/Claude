TKM game - Google reporting TEST (Option B) - version 0.1
================================================================================

This is a small experiment, not the real game. It lets you try Option B, where the game sends each answer
to a Google Sheet in YOUR school Google account by itself, so students never copy or paste a code.

What you get:
- Code.gs                         the script that goes inside your Google Sheet
- test-game.html                  a small practice page you upload to Canvas, like the game
- this READ ME

The practice page has 4 short ORIGINAL passages (not from the novel) with 2 questions each. Each passage has
a pretend chapter number (1 to 4), so you can watch chapters unlock when you change one cell in your Sheet.

Time: about 20 minutes for Parts 1 to 3.

================================================================================
PART 1  MAKE THE SHEET AND ITS SCRIPT (about 10 minutes)
================================================================================

Use your SCHOOL Google account.

1. Open a new Google Sheet: type  sheets.new  in the address bar. Name it, for example, "TKM game results".
2. In the Sheet, click Extensions > Apps Script. A new tab opens with a file called Code.gs.
3. Delete everything in Code.gs. Open Code.gs from this zip (Notepad on Windows, TextEdit on a Mac), copy
   ALL of it, and paste it in. Click the Save icon (the floppy disk).
4. Go back to the Sheet's tab and RELOAD the page. After a few seconds a "TKM game" menu appears at the top.
5. Click TKM game > 1. Set up this Sheet.
   Google asks you to allow access ("Authorization required"): click Continue, pick your account, click
   Allow. If it says "Google hasn't verified this app", click Advanced, then "Go to ... (unsafe)". (It is
   your own script; Google says this about every script a teacher writes.)
   Then click TKM game > 1. Set up this Sheet once more. Four tabs appear: Settings, Students, Answers,
   Summary.
6. In the Students tab, type names in column B, starting in row 2. For the test, put yourself and one or two
   "test students" (for example "Test Student"). Real students can be added later.
7. Click TKM game > 2. Make join codes for new students. Each name gets a code like K7Q-2MF in column A.
8. Make the web link. Back in the Apps Script tab:
   Deploy > New deployment > click the gear next to "Select type" > Web app.
      Description:     TKM test
      Execute as:      Me
      Who has access:  Anyone
   Click Deploy. Copy the "Web app" link (it ends in /exec). Keep it; you need it in Part 2.

   If "Anyone" isn't in the list and you only see "Anyone within [your district]": pick that for now and
   tell me. It probably means students can't send answers from home, and that is an important result.

================================================================================
PART 2  PUT YOUR SHEET'S LINK INTO THE PRACTICE PAGE (2 minutes)
================================================================================

The easiest way: send me the /exec link and I'll send back test-game.html with it already inside.

To do it yourself on Windows:
1. Right-click test-game.html > Open with > Notepad.
2. Press Ctrl+F and search for:   var SCRIPT_URL = "";
3. Paste your link between the two quotation marks, so it looks like:
      var SCRIPT_URL = "https://script.google.com/macros/s/AKfy.../exec";
4. File > Save. Close Notepad.

(Just trying it on your own computer first? Skip this part. Double-click test-game.html and paste the link
into the box it shows. That only works on that computer.)

================================================================================
PART 3  PUT THE PRACTICE PAGE IN CANVAS (5 minutes)
================================================================================

Same steps as the real game:
1. In your course, Files > + Folder (for example "TKM Google test"). Upload test-game.html into it.
2. Click test-game.html once. From the address bar, note COURSE (after /courses/) and NUMBER (after /files/).
3. Make an UNPUBLISHED Page (or use Student View to test). Click </> (HTML Editor) and paste, with your
   numbers:

   <iframe src="/courses/COURSE/files/NUMBER/preview" width="100%" height="650" allowfullscreen="allowfullscreen"></iframe>

4. Save.

================================================================================
PART 4  TRY IT
================================================================================

1. Open the Page. Type a join code from your Students tab (for example the test student's) and click Start.
   The page greets that student by first name and shows chapters 1 to 3.
2. Answer a few questions. The bar at the top says "Sending..." and then "All answers sent".
3. Look at your Sheet:
   - Answers tab: one row for each answer, with the student's name, item, chapter, skill, whether it was
     right on the first try, and the letter picked.
   - Summary tab: % right by student, by chapter and by skill, and the most-missed items (once an item has
     3 or more answers). These update by themselves.
4. Unlock a chapter: in the Settings tab, change "Unlocked through chapter" from 3 to 4. In the practice page,
   click "Check for newly opened chapters" (or reload). The fourth passage appears.
   (In the real game, this replaces the unlock codes in the build instructions.)
5. Try "no internet": turn off the Chromebook's Wi-Fi, answer a question, and see "Waiting to send: 1".
   Turn Wi-Fi back on. Within a minute (or when you click "Send now" under "For the teacher") it is sent.
6. Pause reporting: in Settings, set "Accept answers" to FALSE. Answers then wait on the Chromebook until you
   set it back to TRUE.
7. From home: open the same Page on a Chromebook at home and answer one question. This is the most
   important test, because home and school networks can behave differently.

Students: if you want a few students to try it, add their names, make codes, and give each one their code.
"Not you? Change the join code" at the bottom lets one Chromebook switch students (for testing).

================================================================================
PART 5  WHAT TO LOOK FOR (AND WHAT TO TELL ME)
================================================================================

Please tell me:
- Did answers reach the Sheet from school? From home?
- Did "Anyone" exist in the deployment settings, or only "Anyone within [district]"?
- Did the unlock change show up?
- Anything confusing for students (the join code step, the messages)?
- What you want to see in the Summary tab that isn't there.

If something didn't work, open "For the teacher: what this page knows" at the bottom of the practice page
and copy what it says. The last line is the Sheet's last reply.

================================================================================
PART 6  TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

- "Google sent a sign-in page instead of the Sheet": in Apps Script, Deploy > Manage deployments > pencil
  icon > Who has access: Anyone > Deploy.
- "Can't reach your teacher's Sheet right now": Canvas or a web filter is blocking Google. Run the Canvas
  check page (canvas-check.html) from the same place and send me its results.
- "That code isn't on your teacher's list": check the code in the Students tab (column A). A code you type
  by hand works within a minute.
- No "TKM game" menu: reload the Sheet. If it still isn't there, open Extensions > Apps Script and check
  that Code.gs was saved.
- You changed Code.gs: Deploy > Manage deployments > pencil icon > Version: New version > Deploy. This keeps
  the same /exec link. (A NEW deployment makes a new link, and the practice page would need it.)
- Don't sort or edit the Answers tab while students are playing. Copy it to another tab to work on it.

================================================================================
PART 7  PRIVACY AND CLEANING UP
================================================================================

- Answers go only to this Sheet in your school Google account. Nothing goes to me or anyone else.
- The practice page sends the join code, not the student's name. The name is only in your Students tab.
- The Students tab has real names: share the Sheet only with staff.
- Before using this with a whole class, check with your tech coordinator that a Google Sheet in your school
  account is a fine place for practice results.
- To stop: in Apps Script, Deploy > Manage deployments > Archive. To delete everything, delete the Sheet.
- On a Chromebook, "Forget everything on this Chromebook" (under "For the teacher") clears the practice
  page's saved code and answers.
